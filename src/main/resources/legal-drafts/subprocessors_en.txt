1. Possible recipient roles
Services may involve website/database/backup hosts, API gateways and channel operators, final model providers, payment services, third-party authentication and support. Not every recipient is a subprocessor for every activity; payment or authentication services may independently determine some purposes. Roles require review of actual contracts and activities.

2. Models versus channels
A model brand, display name, group or site label is not a legal entity name or proof of processing country. A request may traverse multiple gateways, and routing, failover or retry may change recipients. Listing model brands alone does not establish that no other third parties are involved.

3. Required verified disclosures
For each actual service, the operator must provide its legal name, purpose, data categories, processing/storage regions, privacy/contract information, retention/training rules, transfer safeguards and change-notice arrangements. Only verified recipients may be listed; do not invent providers, locations, DPAs or certifications.

4. Confirmation before use
If you need a specific region, provider restrictions, subprocessor approval or an enterprise DPA, obtain written route and contract information via the published email. Do not submit sensitive, confidential or localisation-restricted data while the inventory and necessary safeguards remain unconfirmed. Update this page when providers change and give advance notice where law or contract requires.
